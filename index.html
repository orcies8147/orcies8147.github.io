RESEARCH · 2025 — PRESENT↗

SEMAPHORE

Semantic Detour over Incomplete Knowledge Graphs for Multi-hop Numerical QA

Designed a neuro-symbolic framework combining path generation, relation grounding, semantic detours, and dynamic answer synthesis. Evaluated multi-hop numerical QA under missing-triple settings.

Knowledge GraphsNumerical ReasoningNeuro-symbolic AI

SEMAPHORE Interactive Demo

ITRC Human Resource Development Festival · COEX, Seoul

Presented interactive visualizations of graph traversal, relation grounding, semantic detours, 3D RotatE trajectories, and answer synthesis.
